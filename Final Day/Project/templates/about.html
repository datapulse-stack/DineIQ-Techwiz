About the platform

Data Pulse

The heartbeat of your restaurant, in real numbers.

Data Pulse turns raw point-of-sale, inventory and customer records into decisions an owner can act on this week — what to cook, what to price, what to cut and where to grow. No spreadsheets to wrangle, no data-science degree required.

📈

Sees everything

Revenue, profit, orders, wastage, forecasts, pricing, promotions, market-basket and per-location performance — 13 boards from one pipeline.

🧠

Two brains, cross-checked

An independent Spark-style pipeline and a Python pipeline classify every dish, then we compare them so you trust the answer, not a black box.

🎯

Tells you what to do

Every board ends in a plain-English action, ranked by impact — from "cap this promo" to "raise price here, it has headroom".

🔒

Roles that matter

Analyst reads, Manager exports and plans, Admin manages people — each with a genuinely different, permission-enforced experience.

💬

Ask it anything

The built-in assistant answers questions about your numbers in seconds — "what's my top item?", "how bad is wastage?" — straight from live data.

🎨

Built to be lived in

A calm, premium interface with light and dark themes and a restaurant ambience — designed for a busy owner, not a lab.

Why we built it

The story behind Data Pulse

Most restaurants sit on a goldmine of data and never touch it. The reports are too technical, the tools too expensive, and the day is too busy. Data Pulse was built to close that gap: take the same techniques big chains pay analysts for — segmentation, elasticity, demand forecasting, anomaly detection — and hand them to any owner in a form they can read in a single glance and act on before the next service.
